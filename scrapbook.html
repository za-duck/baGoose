Aliza. My silly goose. My favourite person. Meri chand.
I don't know how to begin, because everything I want to say feels both too small and too much at the same time. But here we are, another year, and you deserve every word.

You get overwhelmed, and then you pull through, so beautifully, every single time. You take everyone with you. You collect people and carry them and still somehow show up whole. That's not nothing. That's you.
I adore the way you care. About everyone. Without condition. The way you love people properly, the way you show up for them, the way your heart just has room — it makes me want to be better. And I think, because of you, I am.
I am so Alhamdulillah that you chose me. That it's us (you and me). I don't take that lightly, not for a single day. You are my best friend, my confidant, my heart, meri jaan in the truest sense of the word. And I hope, I genuinely sincerely hope, that I am for you what you are for me. That you feel safe with me, that you feel known by me, that when the world is a lot, I am someone you can rest in.

And I hope, In Sha Allah, that I'm there for all of it — holding your hand, or maybe just your finger, your thumb, your pinky.
Thank you for being my favourite.
Thank you for choosing me.
Thank you for being my Aliza.
